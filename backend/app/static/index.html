What it does

Every role, captured

A new recruitment email arrives and is picked up automatically. One email describing four vacancies becomes four job records, each with company, position, salary, hours, location and requirements pulled out by meaning rather than by matching labels.

Nothing invented

If the salary was not stated, it stays unstated. Anything ambiguous or low-confidence goes to a review queue instead of being guessed at, so your people check the exceptions rather than processing every message. False data costs more than missing data.

A dataset that compounds

Every role captured is one more row of evidence about what clients pay, which skills keep appearing and which vacancies will not fill. Ordinary daily work becomes the record your competitors are throwing away.

How it works

Connect Outlook. That is the setup.

  1. 01

    Sign in with Microsoft and grant read-only access to the mailbox that receives recruitment mail.

  2. 02

    Choose how far back to start — today, the last few days, or a date you pick. We do not pull your whole mailbox.

  3. 03

    New mail is processed as it lands. Review what needs a human, correct anything wrong, export to Excel when you need it.

Security

Read-only, and provably so.

We request Mail.Read only. expressautomate cannot send, modify or delete email — the permission to do so is never granted.
Each agency's data is isolated in the database itself, not only in application code. Tokens are encrypted at rest.
The original email is never discarded. Every extracted field keeps the wording it came from, so any record can be traced back.
